## Creative-connection seeds (unexplored crossings, in rough order of promise)

- **Mergeable evidential memory × federation.** Forward-decay banks + one-sided
  sketches merge by addition (CRDT-like). Nobody has built a *federated evidential
  memory* where many small devices contribute evidence with certified counts and
  Dawid–Skene per-device reliability. This composes five verified findings into
  something with no published neighbor — and it is squarely on mission (many small
  machines ≈ one large memory).
- **Provenance polynomials × distillation.** Distill (answer, top-k provenance) pairs
  so the student inherits *citations*, not just logits — the missing
  provenance-preserving distillation, made concrete by the exact ledger.
- **Conformal abstention × speculative decoding.** Speculative decoding verifies
  drafts losslessly; a conformal evidential gate could verify *claims* the same way —
  draft-with-cheap-memory, verify-with-expensive-retrieval only where coverage is low.
  Surprise economy, applied to honesty.
- **Conceptors × relation contexts.** Jaeger's conceptor algebra (Boolean AND/OR/NOT
  on soft projections) as the context-gating layer over banks — training-free
  composition of relational contexts, and an interference shield for continual updates.
- **Sheaf Laplacians × the evidence graph** (flagged "adopt later" by the framework
  audit): harmonic residual as a differentiable global-consistency score across banks —
  a conflict detector that sees structure, not just per-key disagreement.
- **ACT-R × modern evidential heads.** Fit ACT-R's closed-form retrieval calculus to
  the same battery as the Gram head; 40 years of cognitive-psych parameter estimates
  become priors, and any gap between the two is publishable either way.
- **Test-time compute × the workspace.** Frontier "thinking longer" = more tokens.
  REFA's GRU workspace could think longer by *iterating over memory reads* (VTE-style
  deliberation cycles, uncertainty-gated) — latent test-time compute priced in memory
  reads, not tokens. Connects to Waves P7 (itinerancy) without the wave commitment.
- **Wu–Maass BTSP CAM × product-key slots.** One-shot binary writes into √N-addressed
  slots: a hardware-friendly episodic organ with one-bit synapses — the intersection of
  two verified 2025–26 results that have never met.

## How to run a sweep to this program's standard (the recipe that produced the corpus)

1. **Finders** (parallel, one mission each): verified primary sources, equations in
   plain text, explicit status (convergent/partial/absent/new/speculative), max ~5
   load-bearing claims each, concrete application to the testbed.
2. **Adversarial verifiers** (one per finder): instructed to *refute*; default REFUTED
   when a source can't be located; corrections recorded verbatim.
3. **Completeness critic**: what would embarrass this synthesis by its absence?
4. **Follow-up finders** on the critic's misses.
5. **Leap panel** (≥3 distinct lenses): novel combinations only, each with mechanism,
   math, novelty check (live searches — do them), first experiment sized to available
   hardware, and a kill criterion.
6. **Retraction pass**: fold verifier corrections in; retract loudly; append to the
   sweep corpus and update `../engram-loop-handoff/HANDOFF.md`.
